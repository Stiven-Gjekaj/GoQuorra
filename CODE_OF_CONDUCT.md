# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be civil.
Argue about the code, and not about the person who wrote it.
Assume that somebody who disagrees with you has a reason, and ask what it is.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment free experience for
everybody, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What helps

- Give a reason with an opinion. "This is slower" invites a measurement.
  "This is bad" invites an argument.
- Say what you measured, and how.
- Accept a decision that goes against you, and record why it was made.
- Assume that a person asking a simple question has read what exists and
  did not find their answer in it. If so, that is a fault in the
  documentation.

## What does not

- Personal attacks, insults, and comments about somebody's identity.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing somebody's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately wasting a reviewer's time.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, the pull requests, the discussions, and anywhere
somebody represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer at stivenagostingjekaj@gmail.com.
Every report is reviewed and investigated, and the person who reported it
keeps their privacy.

A maintainer who does not follow this code faces the same consequences as
anybody else, decided by the other maintainers.

## Consequences

A maintainer may edit or remove a comment, a commit, an issue, or a pull
request that does not follow this code, and will say why.
Repeated or serious behaviour leads to a temporary or permanent ban.
